Four signals · one view

Your browser is a black box. We open it.

Chrome's built-in task manager is ugly and engineer-only. uBlock blocks silently — you never get to see it work. Browser Sentinel turns all of it into a dashboard you actually want to open.

⚡

Resource Assassins

CPU · MEMORY · BATTERY

Every heavy tab shows up as a "wanted card" with a bounty on its head. Spot the one melting your laptop and take it out in one click.

🕵

Tracker Radar

PRIVACY · BLOCKING

See exactly who's watching — the company behind each tracker, how many times they've hit you today, and a one-tap block to fight back.

📊

Traffic Monitor

DATA · USAGE

A "glutton leaderboard" of which sites eat your bandwidth. Set alerts, get a monthly recap — "you fed YouTube 12GB this month."

🌐

Network Pulse

SPEED · QUALITY

Passive monitoring by default — no bandwidth wasted. Each page gets a "detective report": who slowed you down, and by how long.
